About ProAds

ProAds is marketing software made by EzDevSoft, a software company in the United Arab Emirates. It helps businesses see which posts and ads bring people who actually pay, and teaches Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and TikTok to find more of them.

What ProAds does

  • Reads a business's real sales (Shopify, Stripe, or an uploaded orders file) and shows real profit, refunds, and which discount codes or products lose money.
  • Matches each paid order to the post or ad that brought it, using tracking links and the ad platforms' own click IDs.
  • Sends verified, paid sales back to Meta and TikTok through their official APIs, so their ads learn who buys rather than who clicks.
  • Builds ad campaigns from a business's own products and results, and suggests budget changes that only happen when the owner approves them.
