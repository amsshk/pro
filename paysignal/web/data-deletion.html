ProAds

Delete your data

You can delete your ProAds account and everything stored with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Delete it yourself (instant)

  1. Sign in at pro.ezdevsoft.net/app.
  2. Open Connect & upload.
  3. At the bottom, under Your data, click Delete my account and data.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: business name, email, phone, website and password.
  • Every sales and refund record you uploaded or connected.
  • Your post statistics, tracking links and promo codes.
  • Any connected Shopify store's access.

Events already sent to Meta for ads measurement are held by Meta under its own policies; you can manage them in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Can't sign in?

Contact us and we'll delete your data after confirming the request comes from the account's email.
