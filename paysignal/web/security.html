Security at ProAds

ProAds handles sales records and access to advertising accounts, so we keep its access narrow and its data locked down. This page explains how, in plain words.

What ProAds can and can't do

  • Stripe: only restricted, read-only keys are accepted. A full secret key (which can move money) is refused.
  • Shopify: read access to orders (and, if granted, customers and products). ProAds can't change your store, prices or payouts.
  • Meta and TikTok: ProAds only acts on the ad accounts you choose, and only publishes or changes budgets after you approve.
  • You can disconnect any of these at any time, from ProAds or from the platform itself.

How data is protected

  • All traffic uses HTTPS, enforced with HSTS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. Sign-in tokens are stored hashed and expire after 30 days; changing your password ends every other sign-in.
  • Access tokens and keys for connected platforms are encrypted at rest and never shown again after you save them. They are removed from server logs and error messages.
  • Each business's data is kept separate, and every request is checked against the business it belongs to.
  • Connect links for Facebook and Shopify only work in the browser that started them, so nobody can attach your accounts to theirs by sending you a link.
  • Repeated wrong passwords or keys are blocked, without locking out the real owner.
  • Customer emails, phone numbers and IDs are SHA-256 hashed before they are sent to Meta or TikTok.
  • The AI features see totals and post results only, never customer names, emails, phones or individual payments.
  • Deleting your account deletes all of your business's data in ProAds (we keep only our own invoices to you, as tax law requires).

Report a security issue

If you think you've found a vulnerability, email us at our contact address with the details. Please don't access other people's data or disrupt the service while testing. We'll reply and keep you updated until it's fixed.
